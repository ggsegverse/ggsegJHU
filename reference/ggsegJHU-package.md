# ggsegJHU: JHU White Matter Atlases for the 'ggseg' Ecosystem

JHU and ICBM white matter atlases for the 'ggseg' ecosystem. Includes
JHU probabilistic tracts (Hua et al. 2008), ICBM-DTI-81 white matter
labels (Mori et al. 2005), and ICBM tract meshes. Provides unified
'ggseg_atlas' objects with 3D mesh geometry, for use with 'ggseg3d'.
