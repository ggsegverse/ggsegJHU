# ggsegJHU: JHU White Matter Atlases for the 'ggseg' Ecosystem

JHU and ICBM white matter atlases for the 'ggseg' ecosystem. Includes
the JHU probabilistic tract atlas (Hua et al. 2008), built from its
probabilistic maps as a tract atlas with a centerline per tract, and the
ICBM-DTI-81 white matter labels (Mori et al. 2005). Both are
coregistered to the FreeSurfer 'cvs_avg35_inMNI152' template so 2D
slices show cortical reference outlines, and ship as 'ggseg_atlas'
objects with 3D geometry for use with 'ggseg3d'.
